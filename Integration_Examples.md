**Integration:** EmotiBit + TouchDesigner + Python/ML

**Overview:** Uses physiological signals from EmotiBit to generate interactive visual feedback.

**How EmotiBit is used:** Real-time physiological signals are processed and translated into visual representations.

## Pneunocchio

**Integration:** EmotiBit + pneumatic controller + inflatable wearable

**Overview:** Transforms changes in physiological activity into physical movement through an inflatable wearable.

**How EmotiBit is used:** Heart rate and electrodermal activity are monitored to trigger pneumatic inflation.
